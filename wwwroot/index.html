A few good questions.

Is macofu just about backend development?+

No. Macofu means “Make coding fun again.” The vision is full-stack software engineering, from interfaces and application logic to data, cloud, and delivery. The backend factory is our first module, with two playable parts.

Are all 20+ modules available?+

Not yet. A library of 20+ modules is our ambition, rather than a finished catalog. You can play the order dispatch factory today. The broader topics on this page describe the direction we want to take.

How much experience do I need?+

The first module assumes you’re new to backend systems. It introduces terms as you need them and starts with a clear action. You can operate the factory before opening the C# source to look closer.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. The current module runs in your browser, with real requests handled by a running server. You can use buttons instead of dragging, and the lesson supports keyboard navigation, narrow screens, and reduced motion.

Does the first module save my progress?+

Your browser has its own factory. Reloading keeps your progress, and tabs in the same browser share it. Switching parts or restarting clears only your factory. Progress ends after two hours away, if you clear cookies, or when the app restarts. No account is needed.
